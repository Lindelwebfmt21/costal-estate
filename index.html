Who We Are

Your Property, Looked After
While You're Away

Own property on the Namibian coast but live elsewhere? We provide reliable property management and home watch services for absent landlords in Swakopmund, Langstrand, Dolphin Beach and Walvis Bay.

From routine property inspections and maintenance coordination to keyholding, property checks and assistance with local service providers, we help protect your investment while you are away. Whether you own a holiday home, permanent residence or rental property, we provide dependable local support and clear communication, giving you greater peace of mind.
