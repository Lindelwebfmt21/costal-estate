Your Property, Looked After
While You're Away

Own property on the Namibian coast but live elsewhere? We provide reliable property management and home watch services for absent landlords in Swakopmund, Langstrand, Dolphin Beach and Walvis Bay.

What We Do

Our Services

A comprehensive property management solution designed specifically for absentee landlords and property owners with investments along Namibia's coastal region. Our services include maintenance coordination & scheduling, owner reporting, property inspections & home watch, keyholding & access management & rental property support.

Property Management

Comprehensive, owner-focused management of your property, ensuring its upkeep, efficient operations and long-term value while providing owners with peace of mind, whether near or away.

Maintenance Coordination & Scheduling

Coordinating routine maintenance, repairs and service providers to keep your property in excellent condition.

Owner Reporting

Providing regular updates, maintenance reports and clear communication to keep property owners informed and in control.

Property Inspections & Home Watch

Conducting scheduled property inspections to monitor condition, identify potential issues and safeguard your investment.

Keyholding & Access Management

Securely managing property keys and coordinating authorised access for owners, tenants and service providers.

Rental Property Support

Supporting landlords with tenant coordination, property readiness and rental-related operational requirements.

Our Framework

A Methodology Built for
Exceptional Assets

We understand the challenges of owning property from a distance. Whether you live elsewhere in Namibia or abroad, our approach is built around dependable local support, proactive care, and transparent communication.

01

Personalised Property Care

Every property and owner has different needs. We take the time to understand your priorities and tailor our services accordingly.

02

Proactive Management

We believe that identifying potential issues early can help prevent costly repairs and unnecessary stress. We help keep your property secure, well-maintained, and ready for your return.

03

Transparency and Communication

Trust is at the heart of our service. We keep property owners informed through clear updates, inspection reports, photographs, and prompt communication when attention is required.

04

A Trusted Local Presence

Serving Swakopmund, Langstrand, Dolphin Beach, and Walvis Bay, we provide a dependable local point of contact for absent landlords and property owners.
